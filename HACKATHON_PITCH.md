---

## 💡 THE PROBLEM (45 seconds)

Here's a shocking statistic: **76% of Indians don't track their expenses regularly.**

Why? Because:
- Existing apps are too complicated
- Dollar-based apps don't resonate with us
- Generic budgets don't account for Indian expenses – groceries, OTT subscriptions, UPI payments
- We can't afford financial advisors

And here's the bigger issue: **70% of Indian households are joint families**, but there's NO good solution for family finance management.

---

## 🌟 OUR SOLUTION (60 seconds)

**RupeeWise** combines three powerful technologies:

1. **🤖 RAG-Powered AI Assistant** – Ask anything about your money in plain English or Hindi. "How much did I spend on groceries this month?" – Instant answer.

2. **📱 Real-Time Anomaly Detection** – Our system instantly flags unusual spending. Spend ₹50,000 on shopping when your average is ₹3,000? You'll know in seconds.

3. **👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family Finance** – The only app that lets families budget together, set allowances, and track spending across 10 family members.

---

## 🏗️ ARCHITECTURE (45 seconds)

We're running a modern, scalable architecture:

- **Frontend**: React + Vite + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS
- **Backend**: FastAPI + Kafka + Pathway Engine
- **AI**: Gemini 2.5 Flash with RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)
- **Database**: Supabase (PostgreSQL with Realtime)
- **Payments**: UPI-style demo interface

**The magic?** Our Pathway-inspired streaming pipelines process transactions in real-time, detect anomalies with Z-score analysis, and trigger instant alerts.
